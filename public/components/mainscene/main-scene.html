Game Rules
Combat phases
Prepare for the Battle • When the battle begins, you get time to equip/select your gear, and position your character.
No item can be used during this phase. Press the READY button to begin the battle.
Battle • The battle is turn-based. Turn order is determined by the character initiative. Each action consumes action points.
On your turn, you can move your character to any available position controlled by your party paying 4 action points.
You can attack enemies or heal/buff yourself or an ally by selecting the appropriate item and clicking on the enemy or ally.
Such actions cost the action points specified in the item use cost.
Provision items cannot be used during this phase. Equipping or unequipping gear costs no action points.
Press the WAIT button to move to the end of the turn order.
Press the END TURN button to end your turn in the current round.
Resting • Once the battle is over, no items can be used except provisions.
You can press I'M DONE to take your share and leave the dungeon.
Otherwise, press NEXT to prepare for the next dungeon level.
Items
Close-range assault weapons. Takes up one or two weapon slots.
Must be equipped and selected before use in combat.
Shields. Takes up one weapon slot.
Must be equipped to take effect.
Long-range assault weapons. Takes up two weapon slots.
Are not effective in close combat.
May require ammunition. Must be equipped and selected before use in combat.
Ammunition items. Each item matches the weapon by its kind.
Must be equipped in the ammo slot if the weapon requires ammunition of a specific kind.
Close-range assault magic.
Must be selected before use in combat.
Long-range assault magic.
Must be selected before use in combat.
Support magic. Modifies or restores ally attributes. (ex.: health, stamina, agility, endurance, etc.)
Must be selected before use in combat.
Recovery items. Modifies or restores ally attributes. (ex.: health, stamina, agility, endurance, etc.)
Must be selected before use in combat.
Armor. Takes up one of the following slots.
Must be equipped to take effect.
Provision. Restores ally attributes. (ex.: health, mana, etc.)
Can only be used in the "Resting Phase".
Item attributes
Requirements • What is required from your character to be able to equip or use an item. (ex.: strength, agility, intelligence, etc.)
Use Cost • What is required to use an item. (ex.: mana, stamina, action points, etc.)
Range • The reach of an item. An action can be performed from any purple square on any green square.
• Close-range example. You can reach only targets in the adjacent column.
• Long-range example. You can reach any targets except those in the adjacent column.
Spread • An item effect spread. The effect is duplicated from an orange square to the green ones.
• Affects adjacent targets in the same column.
Damage • The damage type(s) an item can deal. (ex.: stabbing, cutting, fire, poison, etc.)
Damage may have a base chance (%). Temporal damage has a duration (in rounds).
Modification • Recovers and/or modifies various character attributes. (ex.: health, stamina, agility, strength, luck, etc.)
Temporal modification has a duration (in rounds). Equipped items have a permanent modification effect.
Price • An item's price.
Purchase Price • The price to sell the item back. Check the sell checkbox to sell an item from your inventory.
Repair Price • The price to repair an item. Check the repair checkbox to repair an item in your inventory.

Icons
• An item cannot be equipped (requirements are not met) or used (not enough action points, stamina, mana, etc.)
• An item is broken and cannot be equipped (wear out is reached durability)
• Action points.
• The character is stunned and skips their current turn.
• The character has depleted their stamina. The damage they take causes critical damage.

Game mechanics
Critical Damage • Doubles the damage.
Critical Miss • If a character's attack misses and their stress is greater than zero, a critical miss check is performed.
The character may damage itself with double damage.
Exhaustion • Any instant physical damage absorbed by a character's equipment accumulates as exhaustion damage.
However, it cannot be enhanced by the opponent's intelligence.
If a character is hit while totally exhausted (stamina is zero), critical damage is dealt.
Stun • Any instant physical damage can cause stun.
When stunned, a character loses its turn in the current round and appears at the end of the turn order in the next round.
If a character is hit while stunned, critical damage is dealt and the stun is reset.
Retreat • If the stress attribute is greater than zero, before each character's turn the retreat check is performed.
A character can miss its turn by moving to a corner of the battlefield.
Equipment Wearout • Each successful action followed by damage, or each action that requires ammunition, increases the wearout of the character's weapon, as well as the wearout of the target's equipment. If the wearout of an item reaches its durability, the item can no longer be used and becomes unequipped.
